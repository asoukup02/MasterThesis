\section{Compensation}
To compensate for the wind influence on the airship a disturbance feedforward controller is implemented. The goal is to extend the controller to

\begin{equation}
	u = u_{PID} + u_{comp}
\end{equation}

where $u_{comp}$ compensates for the forces created by the prevailing wind $\Delta F_w$, i.e. fulfilling 

\begin{equation}
	\Delta F_w = \Delta F_{prop}(u)
\end{equation}

Looking at the dynamical model of the airship one sees that the wind velocity, which directly influences the airspeed via 
\begin{equation}
	V_a = V_b - V_w
\end{equation}
